## 1. Executive Summary

**Leylak Tech** is an independent, full-spectrum digital product studio and software engineering firm headquartered in Cybercity, Ebene, Mauritius. 

Founded on the principle that modern businesses should not have to piece together fragmented tech stacks or coordinate disparate agencies, Leylak Tech delivers end-to-end digital solutions under one roof. The studio combines strategic product thinking, high-end UI/UX design systems, and mission-critical engineering to build ultra-fast web applications, mobile platforms, enterprise cloud architectures, IoT hardware telemetry, and custom AI automations.

In addition to bespoke engineering for startups and enterprise clients worldwide, Leylak Tech actively designs and develops its own proprietary suite of SaaS and AI products targeting high-friction industries such as telephony, gym management, healthcare queuing, and fuel retail.

### 4.1 Web Architecture & Development
Ultra-fast, visually captivating, and conversion-optimized websites and web applications built using modern front-end standards.
- **Full-Stack Web Applications:** Built on **Next.js 14+ (App Router)**, **React**, and **TypeScript**.
- **UI/UX Design Systems:** End-to-end design systems in **Figma** with auto-layout, tokenized variables, responsive breakpoint logic, and developer-mode handoff annotations.
- **Conversion-Driven Landing Pages:** High-performing pages featuring scroll-triggered micro-interactions, Framer Motion animations, and WebGL/Canvas shader effects.
- **SEO & Core Web Vitals:** Strict server-side rendering (SSR), dynamic metadata generation, optimized font delivery, and automated XML sitemaps.

### 4.2 Mobile Engineering (iOS & Android)
Native and cross-platform mobile apps engineered for fluid 60/120fps responsiveness, offline resiliency, and global store deployment.
- **Native iOS:** Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, CoreData, and Apple Developer ecosystem tooling.
- **Native Android:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room Database, Retrofit, and Material Design 3.
- **Cross-Platform:** React Native and Expo for fast-to-market applications with unified codebases and native bridge modules.
- **Features:** Real-time push notifications (Pusher/FCM), biometric security (FaceID/Fingerprint), in-app purchases, offline caching, and live GPS/telemetry routing.

### 4.3 Custom Enterprise Software & Cloud Systems
Bespoke operational tools, ERPs, CRMs, and high-concurrency systems engineered to replace clunky legacy software.
- **Multi-Tenant SaaS Architectures:** Scalable cloud platforms designed with isolated tenant environments, subscription billing engines, and granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- **High-Throughput APIs & Microservices:** Sub-millisecond RESTful and GraphQL APIs developed in **Node.js**, **Go (Golang)**, and **Python**.
- **Database Engineering:** High-performance relational database design using **PostgreSQL** with connection pooling, automated read replicas, zero-downtime migrations, and **Redis** in-memory caching.
- **Hardware & IoT Telemetry:** Custom hardware bridges linking physical appliances (fuel station dispensers, gym turnstiles, biometric gates) directly to cloud backends via WebSockets and MQTT.
- **DevOps & Cloud Security:** Containerized deployments using Docker and Kubernetes, continuous integration (CI/CD) pipelines, automated error tracking, and APM observability.

### 4.4 Artificial Intelligence & Workflow Automation
Implementing state-of-the-art machine learning models and automated pipelines to unlock unprecedented business efficiency.
- **Realtime Multimodal Voice AI:** Voice-driven conversational receptionists and web assistants utilizing the Gemini Multimodal Live API and WebSocket audio streams.
- **Autonomous RAG & Retrieval Pipelines:** Vector database integrations (e.g., PostgreSQL `pgvector`) for contextual question-answering over private corporate knowledge bases.
- **Workflow Orchestrations:** Eliminating manual back-office tasks by syncing data flows across CRMs, ERPs, accounting software, and messaging channels (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack).

### 4.5 E-Commerce Solutions
High-converting storefronts built for scale, seamless repeat purchasing, and multi-gateway regional payment acceptance.

### 6.1 LeySupport — Autonomous Voice AI Receptionist
- **Domain:** Telephony, Customer Service, and Booking Automation
- **Core Function:** An intelligent conversational voice receptionist that answers incoming phone calls, resolves customer inquiries, and schedules appointments 24/7 with zero hold times.
- **Key Features:**
  - Natural human-grade voice conversations over live telephony channels.
  - Automated calendar synchronization (Google Calendar, Outlook, CRM).
  - Live customer lookup and order status reporting.
  - Seamless human escalation when complex intervention is required.

### 6.2 GymLey — Smart Gym Platform & Access Control OS
- **Domain:** Health, Fitness & Facility Operations
- **Core Function:** A complete operational gym management ecosystem pairing administrative control with automated turnstile access and mobile apps.
- **Key Features:**
  - Dedicated **Member App** for QR code turnstile check-ins, subscription management, and workout tracking.
  - Dedicated **Trainer App** for client scheduling, session logging, and personalized workout assignments.
  - Real-time IoT integration with magnetic gates, turnstiles, and biometric readers.
  - Automated recurring membership billing and member churn prevention alerts.

### 6.3 MedLey — Smart Healthcare & Clinic Patient Flow
- **Domain:** Healthcare, Outpatient Clinics & Hospitals
- **Core Function:** An intelligent patient queuing and scheduling platform designed to eliminate crowded waiting rooms and streamline doctor consultation flow.
- **Key Features:**
  - Virtual queue passes issued via live SMS and WhatsApp messages with dynamic wait time updates.
  - Intelligent symptom triage and urgent-care priority routing.
  - Doctor consultation room workload balancing across multiple specialists.
  - Up to 85% reduction in physical waiting room congestion.

### 6.4 FuelEy — Fuel Dispenser Telemetry & Shift Accounting
- **Domain:** Petrol Stations, Fuel Retailers & Energy Logistics
- **Core Function:** Specialized operations software capturing dispenser meter readings and tank levels to balance daily shift books in 1 click.
- **Key Features:**
  - Automated real-time telemetry from fuel pump dispensers.
  - Underground fuel storage tank level and dipstick reconciliation.
  - One-click shift ledger balancing (cash, cards, fleet vouchers, and digital payments).
  - Automated fuel variance, evaporation loss, and leakage detection alerts.

## 8. Client Engagement Methodology

Leylak Tech executes projects through a structured, three-phase delivery framework:

```
┌─────────────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────────────┐
│       PHASE 01          │     │        PHASE 02         │     │        PHASE 03         │
│  Strategy & Discovery   │ ──► │  Design & Architecture  │ ──► │     Build & Scale       │
│ Competitor dissection & │     │ High-fidelity Figma UI  │     │ Clean code, automated   │
│ architecture blueprint  │     │ & microservice modeling │     │ CI/CD & post-launch AI  │
└─────────────────────────┘     └─────────────────────────┘     └─────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Phase 01: Strategy & Discovery**  
   - Detailed analysis of business goals, competitive differentiation, and audience psychology.
   - Creation of the technical blueprint: database schema design, entity relationship diagrams (ERDs), and API specifications.
2. **Phase 02: Design & System Architecture**  
   - Crafting pixel-perfect interactive prototypes in Figma mapping all desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints.
   - Architecture review ensuring database normalization, indexing strategies, and security protocols are locked in prior to development.
3. **Phase 03: Build, Deploy & Scale**  
   - Agile sprints writing modular, thoroughly tested code.
   - Continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines to staging environments for rapid feedback.
   - Post-launch monitoring, database performance tuning, SEO indexing, and integration of automated AI workflows.
